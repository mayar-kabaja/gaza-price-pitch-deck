02 The Problem

Gaza has no digital commerce infrastructure

Our first users: displaced families comparing prices in unfamiliar areas, freelancers and students looking for workspaces and internet, and small business owners who want customers to find them online. These groups have the most urgent need and no alternatives.

No Trusted Price Source

No daily price source, so people walk store to store to compare basic goods.

Constant Displacement

People move constantly, with no way to find nearby places in unfamiliar areas.

No way to find workspaces

Homes lack power and internet, and there's no way to find the work and study spaces that do exist.

No Place to Buy & Sell

No local marketplace, so buying and selling is scattered across Facebook and WhatsApp with no trust.

No directory of stores & products

No single directory of stores and their products, so shoppers can't find who sells what.
